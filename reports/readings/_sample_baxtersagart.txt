p91| 4.1.2 PALATALIZATION
p91| Nonpharyngealized consonants tended to palatalize on the way to Middle Chinese: this
p91| would have had the effect of enhancing the contrast between type A and type B, and
p91| possibly reducing the reliance on the typologically unusual feature of pharyngealiza-
p91| tion. Nonpharyngealized Old Chinese initial alveolar stops and nasals became pala-
p91| tal affricates and nasals in Middle Chinese, unless the palatalization was blocked by
p91| prevocalic *-r-:
