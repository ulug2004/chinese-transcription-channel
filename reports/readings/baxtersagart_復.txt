[On] “反 兮 fǎn xī”: [反 fǎn has its] usual pronunciation [i.e., MC
pjonX]; it means 復 fù ‘to revert’. The Hán 韓 version of the Shī reads
“變 biàn” [MC pjenH], meaning ‘to change’ (JDSW 67).
