  Chinese had a voiced b- series (並母) and used it. A scribe who wrote an
  m- character was not forced into it by the writing system.

  Bearing on 冒頓 *mək-tuən: the m- is very unlikely to be standing in for
  a b-, which is what reading the name as *baγatur requires. Combined with
  the missing -r (step 14: stop codas do not survive, and no -r is present
  in the reading at all), two independent lines of evidence tell against
  the standard etymology.
