Step 50.  虛閭權渠, and what an unaccounted character costs.

1.  How often does a transcription use more characters than syllables?
======================================================================
   one character to one syllable                   772   75.9%
   fewer characters than syllables (compression)    93    9.1%
   MORE characters than syllables                  152   14.9%

   But that last figure is two different things:
      a Chinese semantic suffix appended           77
          舍衛大城         śrāvastī
          富單那鬼         pūtana
          富多那鬼         pūtana
          布怛那鬼         pūtana
          迦樓羅鳥         garuḍa
          摩揭陀國         magadha
          摩利支天         marīcī
          摩里支天         marīcī
      no obvious suffix: phonetic expansion        75
          多他阿伽度        tathāgata
          多陀阿伽度        tathāgata
          窣路陀阿鉢囊       srotaāpanna
          窣路多阿半那       srotaāpanna
          阿泥底耶         āditya
          訶利帝母         hārītī
          訶利帝喃         hārītī
          阿迦尼瑟吒        akaniṣṭha

   The suffix cases are a category label, not a sound: 鬼 demon,
   鳥 bird, 國 country, 城 city. They do not license leaving a
   character unaccounted in a personal name, because the character
   there is not a Chinese classifier.

   AND 渠 IS NOT SPARE. The same character writes a full syllable at
   且渠 elsewhere in this record. It has a velar onset and an open
   vowel and is doing work there.

3.  kalkan priced on each reading of 虛
======================================================================
   kalkan has back vowels, so its velars are q. Section 8.5 measures
   a back q written with an h- character at 65.2% and with an
   aspirated character at 30.3%.

       虛 = hɨɑ,  a source back q         14.437%   1 in 6
       虛 = kʰɨɑ, a source plain k         0.604%   1 in 165

   Those figures price 虛閭權 only. They say nothing about 渠, which
   is the objection, and they assume 閭 may contribute a bare -l.

   WHAT IS NOT SETTLED
   - No word in the lexicons uses all four characters. If the name is
     one Turkic word, the frame is empty and something is wrong with
     the frame rather than with the search.
   - The three-syllable fits are all -gAn participles, whose suffix
     step 47 established as productive, but none of them reads as a
     ruler's name.
   - kalkan fits only if two of the four characters are nearly idle.
     That is not measured anywhere as a licensed device.
   - 虛 is absent from Schuessler's table under its traditional form
     and present under the simplified 虚, with 戶 撑 祿 脫.
