step 31 - a source liquid inside a consonant cluster

Verified pairs whose source form has a liquid before a consonant: 63
  liquid unwritten                       54  (86%)
  extra character, with an l- onset       6  (10%)
  extra character, no l- onset            3  (5%)

Reading: Later Han has no liquid coda, so a source -rC- can only be
represented by spending a further character on an l- syllable. Where the
character count does not exceed the source syllable count, no character was
spared and the liquid is unwritten. That is the step the Körklü reading of
谷蠡 asks for at 谷 kok = körk.

Caveat: 'extra character' is inferred from counts, not from an alignment of
each character to each syllable, so a pair that compresses elsewhere and
expands here can be misclassified. The unwritten figure is therefore a floor.
