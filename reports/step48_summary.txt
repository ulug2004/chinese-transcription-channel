5.  What the search returns
======================================================================
   The threshold that retires Baγatur in the paper is 1 in 37.
   One candidate clears it with a sense worth having: toña, at 1 in 20,
   on the FIRST tabulated reading of 車, so no polyphony argument is
   needed. Kāşgarî's own entries, on two different pages:

      toña        bebür, kaplan cinsinden bir hayvan; kişi adı   III, 368
      toñalamak   yiğit ve kuvvetlilerin yaptığı işi yapmak      III, 405

   The first records it as a personal name, in the eleventh-century
   dictionary itself and not in any modern reconstruction. The second
   is a verb derived from it meaning to do what heroes and strong men
   do, which puts the heroic sense in the source independently of the
   comparison, on a different page from the noun.

   NOT CHECKED: toña is the epithet in the name Alp Er Toŋa, whom
   Kāşgarî elegises and later tradition identifies with Afrasiab.
   The index searched here carries headwords only, so that cannot be
   confirmed from it. It must be checked in the Dīwān itself before
   being used, and it is not needed: the two entries above stand alone.

   The other cheap forms are not names: kalñu floating on water,
   közñü a mirror, koñuz a dung beetle, karga a crow, köñül the heart.
