  VERDICT for 若鞮: an open character after a stop-final source
  syllable happens in roughly 30% of cases here — common, not
  exceptional. So 鞮 *te following *inak- is an ordinary spelling,
  and the objection that a scribe 'would have closed the syllable'
  does not hold: often he did not. Compare the nasals in the rows
  above, which were closed far more consistently — the same split
  step 14 found between nasal and stop codas across the period gap.
