HOW TO READ THIS, and what it is not:
  * Forms are in ASJP notation (C=ch, S=sh, N=ng, 3=schwa, E=ae). Probabilities
    are normalised over the retained beam, so they are RELATIVE rankings, not
    absolute posteriors.
  * The prior is language-neutral by design. It does not favour Turkic,
    Yeniseian, Mongolic or anything else, and no attribution is implied.
  * The syllable inventory is Sanskrit-derived, so segments Sanskrit lacks -
    q, front-rounded vowels - cannot appear. See the reachability ceiling; it
    caps how often the true form can be produced at all.
  * Read the top-k accuracy on Baley and Uyghur as the honest measure of how
    much to trust any single line above.
