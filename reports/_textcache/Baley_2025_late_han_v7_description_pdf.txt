Abstract
This document describes the content of the columns of the datasets, and offers a quick guide to the
transcriptions’ locations.
1 Columns
This section describes the columns found in the spreadsheet. Most of them are taken from ( Hill, Nattier,
Granger, & Kollmeier , 2020) and re-use the column name.
• A: Translator: name of the translator; this is to be understood as a convenient shortcut for the
whole translator team behind a specific text.
• B: Location: location of the Chinese word in the corpus; see ”Location guide” section below for
further information.
• C: Sanskrit: the Sanskrit word that is understood to be closest to the Chinese transcription for
the given passage, i.e. what would the source text word have been if the source text had been in
Sanskrit.
• D: Pāli: same as C but for Pāli.
• E: Gāndhari: same as C but for Gāndhari.
• F: Chinese: the Chinese word as found in the corpus.
• G: Schuessler Late Hàn 2007/2009: Reconstructed Late Hàn pronunciation of the Chinese characters
found in the word, based on ( Schuessler, 2007) and ( Schuessler, 2009).
• H: Coblin’s Reconstruction: Reconstructed Late Hàn pronunciation of the Chinese characters found
in the word, as found in ( Coblin, 1983).
• I: Middle Chinese: Middle Chinese notation following the system found in ( Baxter, 1992).
• J: Pinyin: Modern Standard Chinese pinyin (for illustration only).
• K: Coblin 1983 Glossary number: for entries of the dataset that were present in ( Coblin, 1983), the
entry number in the book.
• L: Karashima 2010 Glossary Loc. (page): page of ( Karashima, 2010) that contains the Chinese
word. In ”163.ii”, ii means it is the second entry on page 163.
• M: Karashima (Krsh) location: in ( Karashima, 2010), the author references his other publications
for further reading. His abbreviations are somewhat unintuitive, especially considering his prolicif
academic output:
– Krsh: ( Karashima, 1992) (”Krsh” appears without a date for this reference, but this is ex-
plained in ( Karashima, 2010, xxxvi))
– Krsh(1998): ( Karashima, 1998)
– Krsh(2001): ( Karashima, 2001)

• N: English translation (Karashima): the English gloss and comment of the entry in ( Karashima,
2010).
• O: Nattier notes: Comments found in the ( Hill et al. , 2020) dataset.
2 Location guide
The Location column contains references to printed texts and manuscripts; those are described in detail
in the main article. The section below provides a quick reference to the texts.
• ”KA”: the Ānbān shǒuyì jīng 安般守意經 manuscript from the Kongō-ji 金剛寺 temple (KA =
Kongō-ji Ānbān). Column numbers are quoted according to the Kongō-ji ’A’ manuscript.
• ”TG”: the Twelve Gates manuscripts: Shí’èr mén jīng 十二門經, Jiě shí’èr mén jīng 解十二門經,
and an anonymous commentary on the two previous texts. Column numbers are quoted according
to the Kongō-ji ’A’ manuscript.
• ”T”: Taishō Shinshū Daizōkyō 大正新脩大藏經 (Taishō Revised Tripitaka). A reference such as
T13.238b23 indicates the 13 th work (commonly referred to as T13; it is found in vol.1, although we
do not record this information in the dataset), page 238, middle section (’b’), column 23.
