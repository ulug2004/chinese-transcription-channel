Step 52.  Word-final consonants, and a blind spot in the Later Han corpus.

======================================================================
1.  Why the Sanskrit corpus cannot answer this
======================================================================
   pairs                                   1017
   of those, the source word ends in a consonant     51  (5.0%)

   That is a structural blind spot rather than a small sample: the
   source language does not present the problem. Turkic presents it
   constantly, so a Turkic reading ending in a consonant depends on
   a device the paper's main corpus cannot price.

======================================================================
2.  What the Turkic corpus shows
======================================================================
   consonant-final Turkic words                    380  100.0%
     written with an OPEN final character          244   64.2%
     written with a CLOSED final character         136   35.8%

   Two thirds of the time the last character is open, which means
   the final consonant is either unwritten or carried by a
   character whose vowel is doing nothing.

   of those, with an EXTRA final character         199  100.0%
     the extra character writes that consonant      91   45.7%
     the extra character does something else       108   54.3%

   Examples of the device the three rows use:
       abïnus         a-pi-nou-sseu              extra character sseu
       ayïz           a-hei-sseu                 extra character sseu
       almas          ngan-ma-sseu               extra character sseu
       altmïs         ngan-ti-mi-che             extra character che
       as             a-sseu                     extra character sseu
       as             a-che                      extra character che
       az             a-sseu                     extra character sseu
       ärmäz          a-eul-ma-sseu              extra character sseu
       ärür           ngo-lou-eul                extra character eul
       baqïr          pa-hei-eul                 extra character eul

   sseu for a final -s or -z, che for -ş, eul for -r. The character
   carries the consonant and its own vowel is epenthetic.

======================================================================
3.  What this licenses, and what it does not
======================================================================
   LICENSED. A Chinese character whose onset writes a word-final
   consonant, its vowel writing nothing, is a real and common device
   in Turkic transcription. 侯 at 骨都侯, 閭 at 虛閭權渠 and 姑 at
   狐鹿姑 are instances of it, not irregularities.

   NOT LICENSED. This does not license leaving a character
   unaccounted altogether, which is the separate objection at
   虛閭權渠, where 渠 writes neither a consonant nor a syllable.

   NOT SETTLED. The corpus is Ming. The matching table used here is
   coarse, so the 'does something else' row is an upper bound on the
   failures rather than a measured rate; some of those cases are
   probably the same device with a final my table does not list.
